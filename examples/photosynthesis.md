# Photosynthesis

Plants make their own food using sunlight, water and carbon dioxide.
It happens in the chloroplasts, which contain a green pigment called
chlorophyll. Chlorophyll absorbs light energy.

## The equation

carbon dioxide + water -> glucose + oxygen, with light energy.
The glucose is used for growth and stored as starch. The oxygen is
released through the stomata.

## What affects the rate

Light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration and temperature all
change how fast photosynthesis happens. These are called limiting
factors. Past a certain point adding more light stops helping,
because something else has become the limit.
