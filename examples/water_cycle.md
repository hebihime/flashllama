# The Water Cycle

Water moves between the oceans, the air and the land in a loop
powered by the sun.

## Evaporation and condensation

The sun heats water in oceans and lakes and it evaporates into
water vapor. As the vapor rises it cools and condenses into tiny
droplets, which form clouds.

## Precipitation and collection

When the droplets get heavy enough they fall as rain, snow or
hail. The water collects in rivers, lakes and oceans, and the
cycle starts again.
